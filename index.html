Spring 2026  /  Machine Learning II

Forward 12-Month Drawdown Prediction

Dual-stream neural network

Forecasts the maximum peak-to-trough drawdown a US public company will experience over the next twelve months. The model reads five years of Compustat accounting ratios through an LSTM and seven CRSP-derived price features through an MLP, then fuses the two streams into a single forecast bounded in [−1, 0].

  • MAE 0.121  RMSE 0.161  R² 0.410
  • PR-AUC 0.852 at −30% drawdown
  • 0.666 within-year Spearman
  • 0.487 top-decile precision

Evaluated on a 2020–2023 test fold of 15,311 firm-years with a 3-seed ensemble. Beats the volatility-only baseline on every primary metric.

TableSight

Hierarchical table extraction

A side-by-side dashboard for pulling structure out of table images: drop one in, see what three different approaches do with it, and score them against ground truth. Florence-2, a vision-language model LoRA fine-tuned on FinTabNet. UniTable, a three-pass encoder-decoder that predicts table structure, then bounding boxes, then per-cell text. Winter 2026  /  Bayesian ML

Bayesian DJ

Recommendation under uncertainty

Most recommenders treat your taste as a fixed point estimate. This one treats it as a probability distribution and updates it live. You describe a mood in plain English ("late night drive, something dark and smooth"), which becomes informative Gaussian priors over nine Spotify audio features. Play and skip feedback refines those into a posterior via online Bayesian logistic regression with Laplace approximation, and Thompson sampling picks each next track, balancing exploration against what it already knows you like.

Spring 2025  /  UChicago Data Science Clinic

Who actually breathes California's pesticides

California accounts for roughly 20% of national pesticide use, much of it classified high-risk, but the public data is granular and unwieldy. Our team analyzed the California Pesticide Use Report alongside US Census Bureau demographics and the California State Geoportal to find who bears the exposure. Counties with the heaviest high-risk pesticide use skewed toward Hispanic populations, residents without high school degrees, and agricultural workers. School districts in the Central Valley saw the most intense exposure, and 14.9% of California public schools sit within a mile of intense pesticide application areas. Use declined steadily from 2017 to 2022, but the exposure disparities did not.

Decarbonizing a global logistics firm

As part of Team Solar Express, I helped build a quantitative decarbonization strategy for Kuehne and Nagel: hydrotreated vegetable oil and battery-electric trucks for road transport, rooftop solar across warehouses to cover Scope 2 emissions, blue and green methanol for maritime, and a ramp on sustainable aviation fuel.
